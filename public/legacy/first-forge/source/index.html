A FIELD GUIDE

Move. Survive.
Remember the way through.

You are a spark inside a body. The Demiurge’s constructs guard twelve connected chambers. Weapons fire automatically; your job is to move, collect memory fragments, and dash through danger.

Move & dash
WASD or arrow keys to move. Space to dash with a brief moment of invulnerability. On touch screens, use the stick and DASH button. In co-op, hold E near a fallen partner for two seconds to rekindle them; both players must approach a gate to cross.
Choose a gift
Blue fragments level you up. Time stops while you choose one of three powers. Press 1, 2, or 3 on a keyboard, or tap a whole card. Stack weapon damage, attack speed, orbiting sparks, recovery, and movement.
Find a gate
Clear a chamber, make its karma choice, then walk to a gate and press E or tap GATE. Crystal, root, and basalt seals need their matching abilities. M opens the connected map.
Remember across lives
Abilities earned by clearing chambers become permanent after the run ends. Open Equipment & forge between lives. Carry one relic and one ward, enhance items to +3, or buy lasting memories. Starter equipment is free; the first memory costs 4 essence. Karma changes the three bodies offered next.
Reach the Eye
The Machine Sepulcher grants Prism Phase. The Root Cathedral grants Root Passage. Basalt Reach grants Fault Breaker. The Archon Foundry grants the Eye Sigil. Defeat the Warden to open the seam.

The original 108-choice life simulator is preserved in Echo Lives. Its first saved karma seeds your action journey. This is a fictional cosmology.

TWO SPARKS · ONE CURRENT

Online co-op

Start a fresh shared life. Both players fight the same swarm. The host’s equipment bonuses apply to both bodies. The host chooses shared gifts and karma decisions. Stay together at gates; hold E near a fallen partner to revive.

Hosts choose a form from their current karma offers. Joining players may choose any of the six bodies.

Your partner also needs access to this game. Room codes pair players; they do not change the game’s sharing settings. Rooms last up to two hours. Reload this tab to reconnect.
