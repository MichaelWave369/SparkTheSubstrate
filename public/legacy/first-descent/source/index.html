A FIELD GUIDE

Move. Survive.
Remember the way through.

You are a spark inside a body. The Demiurge’s constructs guard nine connected chambers. Weapons fire automatically; your job is to move, collect memory fragments, and dash through danger.

Move & dash
WASD or arrow keys to move. Space to dash with a brief moment of invulnerability. On touch screens, use the stick and DASH button.
Choose a gift
Blue fragments level you up. Time stops while you choose one of three powers. Stack weapon damage, attack speed, orbiting sparks, recovery, and movement.
Find a gate
Clear a chamber, make its karma choice, then walk to a gate and press E or tap GATE. Crystal, root, and basalt seals need their matching abilities. M opens the connected map.
Remember across lives
Abilities earned by clearing chambers become permanent after the run ends. Spend earned essence between lives on lasting improvements. Karma changes the three bodies offered next.
Reach the Eye
The Machine Sepulcher grants Prism Phase. The Root Cathedral grants Root Passage. Basalt Reach grants Fault Breaker. The Archon Foundry grants the Eye Sigil. Defeat the Warden to open the seam.

The original 108-choice life simulator is preserved in Echo Lives. Its first saved karma seeds your action journey. This is a fictional cosmology.
