First Spellcraft lesson · optional Descent guide

Classic fires basic attacks automatically. Spellcraft lets you direct them with your selected casting hand.

  1. Choose Spellcraft here or in the wrist's Combat page. Select left or right casting hand there.
  2. Hold the casting-hand trigger, point toward an enemy, then release. A small gesture is enough; stationary trigger release also works.
  3. Use the other trigger for your vessel's normal power. Its normal recovery still applies.
  4. Aimed shots can miss or hit cover. The aiming mark reports the observed target; wait for normal basic recovery before another cast.
  5. Switch back to Classic whenever you prefer automatic basics. Charge changes the feedback; damage follows the same game rules.

Read this in the hub, then enter or resume your normal journey when ready. This guide grants no progress or rewards. The Arena uses its own directed Duel combat.
